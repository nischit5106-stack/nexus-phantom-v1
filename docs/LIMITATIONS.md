# Limitations

- Construct validity: intervention labels do not identify real manipulation or intent; event histories lack key contextual provenance.
- External validity: selector and planting share a narrow recurring schedule; only population changes, not broad mechanism coverage, are tested prospectively.
- Statistical scope: 42 source groups with dependent arms/replicas; degenerate conditional intervals are not universal zero-error bounds.
- Reconstruction: only two historical holdout blocks; later stress sets reuse development histories, and final detection does not validate the full investigation pipeline.
- Comparative evidence: no matched published-method baseline or demonstrated state-of-the-art superiority.
- Operational evidence: no analyst study, deployment, enforcement validation or production-throughput measure.
- Reproduction: raw records, protected truth and uncleared full research core are absent; public materials support a narrower inspectable reproduction.

The shared-stimulus/shifted-overlap failures and original verifier failure remain disclosed. A scientific change requires PHANTOM v2 and a fresh evaluation; September 2026 cannot become unseen evidence again.
