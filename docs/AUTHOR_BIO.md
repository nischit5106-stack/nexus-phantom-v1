# Author bio

**Nischit Girish Kumar**  
**NEXUS Research Initiative — Independent Research**

Independent researcher. Statistical research. Systems engineering.

Nischit Girish Kumar is an independent researcher working on statistical methods, systems engineering and the limits of behavioral evidence. He directed PHANTOM's architecture, benchmark design, experimental protocols, acceptance gates and validation. His technical work spans Python data engineering, DuckDB/parquet audits, permutation reference models, robust normalization, graph reconstruction and a read-only investigation interface. PHANTOM examines when coordinated-listening evidence supports a structural claim and when additional provenance is needed to interpret its cause.
