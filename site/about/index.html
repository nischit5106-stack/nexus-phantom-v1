PHANTOM began with a question about coordinated manipulation. Its most useful lessons came from finding what a benchmark, statistical score or event stream could not establish.

Technical work.
Research judgment.

I directed PHANTOM’s architecture, benchmark design, experimental protocols, acceptance gates and validation. The work involved Python data engineering, DuckDB/parquet audits, permutation reference models, robust normalization, graph reconstruction and a read-only investigation interface.

AI-assisted tools supported selected engineering tasks, with minor assistance in documentation and manuscript preparation. Nischit Girish Kumar directed the research, system architecture, experimental design, validation, and final technical decisions and assumes responsibility for the work.

What changed my thinking

High scores did not survive several benchmark-validity checks. Later, strong structural evidence still could not identify intent. I narrowed the research claim and changed the system’s output contract instead of treating missing information as a scoring problem.
