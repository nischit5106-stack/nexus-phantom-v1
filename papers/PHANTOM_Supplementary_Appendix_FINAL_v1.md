## A. Research chronology

Objective: Define an event-level research system. Hypothesis: Relational evidence may improve over individual anomaly heuristics.

Method: Architecture, contracts and staged gates. Result: Aspirational specification, not empirical validation.

Gate: Require evidence before stronger claims. Decision and resulting change: Original scope superseded where later falsification narrows it.

## B. Methodological appendix

### B1. Read the two detectors separately

The Phase 4.11 population detector and Phase 5.1 reconstruction core are different methods. The former selects one exact recurring actor set, computes population minute-bin coactivity and uses 4095 null draws. The latter selects singleton-user burst support, constructs a fixed catalog graph and pools component evidence over 511 draws. External ListenBrainz success belongs to the former. Historical internal graph reconstruction belongs to the latter. Their evidence must not be merged into a claim of externally validated end-to-end investigation.

### B2. Population detector pseudocode

1. Read only the detector-visible canonical event array. Group exact timestamps by recurring actor set. Require at least eight distinct users occurring once, exactly 32 recurring timestamps in distinct minute bins, span at least 27 days, and exactly one qualifying group. Otherwise return unsupported.

2. Reduce background to unique (user, item, minute) presences for sufficient counts. Keep background, actor identities and selected timestamps fixed. Independently permute each selected actor's sorted item multiset over its slots using 4095 draws and seed 41101.

3. Compute the same ordered cross-actor coactivity numerator and opportunity denominator for observation and every draw. Center by null median and scale by 1.4826 times MAD. Zero scale maps equal/above/below median to 0/positive sentinel/negative sentinel. Alert at robust excess at least three.

4. Return score and support information. This does not return a Gaussian probability, a fraud probability, a campaign organizer or an estimate of natural-world false positives.

### B3. Reconstruction pseudocode

1. Restrict the input to START=1651363200 and END=1653782400, with END exclusive. Canonicalize unique (user,item,timestamp) atoms; retain multiplicities as description. Ambiguous actor fingerprints produce abstention.

2. Form bursts by consecutive gaps no greater than 600 seconds. Retain bursts having at least eight distinct users and eight singleton-user atoms. Keep actors present in at least eight qualifying bursts, apply that filter once, then keep groups of at least two selected atoms.

3. Build an untimed selected-catalog graph using shared-item count at least three and Jaccard at least 0.10. Connected components of size at least three are a null-invariant candidate family.

4. Independently permute selected items within each actor with 511 draws and seed 55101. Sum same-item same-bin evidence over shared slots, use the minimum 60/300 count per pair, and average over every unordered pair within each candidate, including zeros.

5. Normalize observed and null component scores with their common median/MAD. Compare observed scores to the per-draw maximum across fixed components with the add-one denominator 512. Require robust excess at least three and comparison value at most 0.01.

6. Link admitted components with target Jaccard at least 0.25 and overlap at least 0.5 relative to the shorter period. Preserve constituent scores. Targets shared in two member catalogs need not have direct temporal support. Return evidence and withheld causal attribution.

### B4. Metrics and uncertainty

For a matched predicted member set P and original parent T, precision is intersection size divided by |P|, recall divides by |T|, and F1 is twice the intersection divided by |P|+|T|. The historical evaluator uses one-to-one Hungarian matching by member F1; unmatched parents receive zero. Mean F1 is the average of per-parent scores, not a recomputation from two unrelated means. Target and fragment metrics have their own denominators. The fragmented-regime recovery claim does not describe every campaign type.

World alert burden counts any structural alert in a control world. It is not a real-world false-positive rate because observed users lack verified legitimacy labels and controls encode a research construction. AUC separates score ranking from the frozen threshold. Primary robustness scores preserve original truth under dropout; surviving-truth analyses answer a different question.

Whole-source resampling respects one layer of clustering but does not establish source independence or mechanism coverage. Perfect observed group outcomes produce degenerate percentile intervals. No population zero-error statement is warranted. The illustrative 42-independent-group zero-event upper bound in the report is explicitly hypothetical, not a replacement confidence interval.

### B5. Causal-twin assumptions

The equality-of-output argument requires the same detector-visible input and, for a randomized method, the same randomness law independent of the hidden cause. It does not require the two causal stories to be equally likely. It does not establish posterior odds, or deny that external provenance or well-justified assumptions could identify causes. The six constructed twins demonstrate a limitation of this information setting. Any future provenance extension changes the task and needs new validation.

### F2. AUC and intervals

Pooled AUC is the fraction of all treatment/control score comparisons with treatment greater than control, plus half the tied fraction. Mean block AUC averages the same statistic over 42 groups with equal group weights. Each group has 9 worlds per arm, so its AUC uses 81 comparisons; the pooled AUC uses 142,884 comparisons. These are arithmetic comparison counts, not independent observations.

The fixed threshold yields 378/378 treatment alerts and 0/378 control alerts. Both AUCs are 1.000. Worst-block control burden is 0.0%. The committed 2,000 whole-block resamples, seed 41102, give the conditional AUC and detection intervals [1.000, 1.000], control-mean interval [0.000, 0.000], and equal-block upper 95% control bound 0.0%. Every observed block is perfect, so these degenerate empirical intervals are expected. They do not bound unobserved mechanisms, establish independence across groups or imply a zero natural-population alert probability. No new interval is substituted for the committed procedure.

## H. Construction, notation and implementation audit

### H1. Controlled-pair construction pseudocode

1. Select a source group with the committed eligibility and exclusion rules. Retain the original event window as the common background. The final selection seed is 41290; this algorithm description does not execute selection.

2. For each committed construction seed and level, use the sealed final412 domain mapping to determine enrollment, catalog and timestamp assignments. Construct the declared recurring schedule with 32 slots and eight items repeated four times. Levels specify 32, 24 or 16 synchronized slots.

3. Produce aligned treatment assignments and the collision-safe matched-control reassignment. Preserve actor identity, row counts, per-actor item and timestamp multisets, and the declared duplicate invariants. Reject a failed invariant; a favorable detector score cannot repair it.

4. Keep arm labels and protected parent information outside the detector-visible array. Score the frozen detector on canonical visible inputs only. The labels describe the construction, not real user intent. Matched arms and seed/level replicas retain shared-source dependence.

This is descriptive pseudocode for the frozen construction, not replacement code. Exact ordering, hash domains and random-number calls are committed in the preserved executable mapping.

### H2. Notation checklist

E is the visible event collection; X is the complete permitted input in the causal argument. u and v index actors, i items, t timestamps, b minute bins, and r randomization draws. n_ib counts unique actor presences for one item/bin; N_b counts actor-item presences in the bin; d_ub is the number of distinct items for one actor/bin. The denominator removes same-actor ordered opportunities.

C is the population coactivity statistic. m is its local null median; the scale is 1.4826 times the median absolute deviation. Z is robust excess, not a normal z-test probability. In reconstruction, G is a fixed candidate component, w_uv^(h) counts shared-slot same-item coincidences at width h, and T(G) averages the 60/300 minimum across all unordered actor pairs, including zeros. M^(r) is the maximum normalized candidate score for draw r.

Every threshold and exception in the main methods is descriptive of frozen code. The zero-scale ordering sentinels are not measured effect sizes. The maximum-family comparison is not asserted to prove organic-listening familywise error control. A merged object has constituent evidence, not a newly calibrated merged p-value.

### H3. Computational scope

The source audit uses disk-backed scans; its 704.7 million-event scope is not a detector throughput result. The population detector scores fixed-background sufficient counts without copying every complete null world. Graph reconstruction stores draw-by-actor-by-slot assignments plus draw-by-component statistics. Catalog comparisons can still be quadratic in selected actors. No production latency, memory budget or unrestricted stream coverage was measured.

### H4. Failure-to-inference map

Origin/neutral/catalog/session failures: generated source or representation features could stand in for coordination; reject the benchmark before accepting detection scores. Duplicate mismatch: preprocessing violated the intended controlled comparison; repair collisions before interpreting performance. Raw/global threshold failure: ranking and alert burden are different claims; local normalization is evaluated separately. Bad graph null: catalog connectivity and incorrect reference conditioning can alert on controls; retain fixed candidates and explicit temporal nulls. Hybrid/non-veto failures: more features need measured benefit and must not erase useful evidence. Shock/shifted-overlap failures: natural common stimuli and overlapping structure defeat a broad operating claim. Causal/ranking ambiguity: absent information cannot be repaired by a stronger scalar score; preserve alternatives and equal-priority sets.
